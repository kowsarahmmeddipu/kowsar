Fabric and care

Linen

Breathable and cool. It softens with every wash.

Wash cool, line dry, iron while damp.

Cotton poplin

Crisp, smooth and hard wearing for daily use.

Machine wash at 30°C, iron on medium.

Viscose

Fluid and light, with a soft drape.

Gentle wash, do not wring, iron inside out on low.

Leather

Develops character over time with careful use.

Wipe with a damp cloth, keep away from heat, store in a dust bag.

Delivery, returns and help

Size and fit

Each piece lists the sizes it comes in. If you are between sizes, choose the larger one for a relaxed fit. Compare your measurements in centimetres or inches in the size guide.

Delivery

Orders ship within 2 working days. Delivery inside Dhaka usually takes 1 to 3 days, and the rest of Bangladesh 3 to 5 days. You receive a tracking update once your order ships.

Returns and exchanges

Return or exchange unworn items with tags within 7 days of delivery. Contact us with your order number to start. Refunds go back to your original payment method.

Payment

Pay by cash on delivery, bKash, Nagad or card. All prices are shown in Bangladeshi taka (৳).
